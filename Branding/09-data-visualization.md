# 9. Data Visualization

Nyxor charts should use restrained executive visualization.

- **Blue** = primary business data
- **Cyan** = positive intelligence/signal
- **Violet** = AI-derived information
- **Amber** = attention
- **Red** = critical

Avoid rainbow dashboards.

## Executive Orbit

A proprietary visualization: CEO at the center, surrounded by orbiting domains — **Strategy · People · Finance · Operations · Customers · Risk · Personal**. Their distance/intensity represents current attention requirements.

This can become a highly recognizable Nyxor motif.

---

**Implementation note**: the color semantics here map cleanly onto the palette in `02-core-visual-system.md` (Orbit Blue/Intelligence Cyan/Violet Pulse/Attention Amber/Critical Red) and are directly usable for the dashboard's stat tiles and any future charting. "Executive Orbit" is a net-new, unbuilt visualization concept — no chart library exists in `apps/web` today (confirmed: no dependency for charts/graphs).
