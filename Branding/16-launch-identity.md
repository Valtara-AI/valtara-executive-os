# 16. Launch Identity

A strong launch campaign could center around:

> **RUN YOUR COMPANY. NOT YOUR CHAOS.**

**Supporting line**: Meet vexOS — the Executive Operating System built for leaders who operate at scale.

## Secondary campaign statements

- Your second brain just became operational.
- One intelligence layer for your executive life.
- From information overload to executive clarity.
- Your company has an operating system. Now you do.

The launch motif should feature the V emerging from a network of orbital paths.
