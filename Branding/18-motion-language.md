# 18. Motion Language

Motion becomes part of the identity.

- **Orbit** — nodes rotating slowly
- **Pulse** — intelligence discovered
- **Converge** — multiple sources becoming one answer
- **Flow** — delegated actions moving outward
- **Focus** — irrelevant information fades away

Keep motion sophisticated and restrained: generally **180–400 ms** for UI interactions.

The logo animation can begin with three orbital nodes, converge into the V, and resolve into **Nyxor**.

---

**Implementation note**: the approved landing-page plan already specifies a dependency-free `IntersectionObserver`-based scroll-reveal approach (no `framer-motion`) for "polished but static" v1 interactivity. The 180–400ms timing guidance here applies directly to that implementation's `transition`/`animate-*` Tailwind utilities.
