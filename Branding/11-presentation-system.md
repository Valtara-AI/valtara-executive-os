# 11. Presentation System

Executive decks should use a **Midnight Executive** theme.

## Cover

Large Nyxor mark. Minimal title. Small gradient orbit.

## Slide families

- Title
- Executive statement
- Big number
- Problem/solution
- Product screenshot
- Architecture
- Comparison
- Timeline
- Case study
- Quote
- Closing

Avoid dense consulting-style decoration. A metric slide might simply read:

> **12.4 hrs**
> Executive capacity recovered each week.
