# 7. AI Assistant Surfaces

Give the intelligence layer its own identity: **VEX — Executive Intelligence**

VEX should not visually resemble a generic chat interface.

## Primary modes

- **Ask** — retrieve knowledge
- **Think** — reason through decisions
- **Brief** — summarize complexity
- **Plan** — turn objectives into action
- **Draft** — create communications
- **Delegate** — assign work
- **Analyze** — interrogate data
- **Remember** — retrieve institutional/personal memory

The orbital V becomes VEX's avatar. When processing, the orbital nodes can subtly rotate around the V.

## AI status language

Instead of "Thinking...", use contextual states such as:

- Connecting context…
- Reviewing priorities…
- Analyzing dependencies…
- Building your briefing…

---

**Implementation note**: VEX-OS's actual agent workforce today is multiple named, per-executive agents (e.g. "Inbox Triage," "Calendar Coordinator," "Board Relations" — user-named at onboarding), not a single unified "VEX" assistant persona. Adopting this section fully would mean introducing a new unifying AI identity layered on top of (or replacing the framing of) the existing multi-agent model — a product decision, not just a visual one. The AI status language ("Connecting context…" etc.) is directly reusable in any loading state regardless of that larger decision.
