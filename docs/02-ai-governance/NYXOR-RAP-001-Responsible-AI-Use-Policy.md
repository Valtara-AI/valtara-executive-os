# 1. Purpose

This Responsible AI Use Policy defines acceptable and prohibited uses of Nyxor, the obligations of executive users and enterprise administrators, and the mechanisms by which policy compliance is enforced and monitored. It complements the AI Ethics & Transparency Framework (NYXOR-ETF-001), which defines internal governance principles. This policy governs the external-facing use of the product.

# 2. Acceptable Use

NYXOR is designed and licensed for the following uses:

- Executive productivity: synthesizing information, drafting communications, preparing briefings, conducting research, tracking delegated work
- Professional delegation: assigning knowledge work tasks to AI agents within the executive's domain of authority
- Decision support: generating structured analysis, options comparisons, and research summaries to inform executive decisions
- Calendar and communication management: reviewing, drafting, and (with HITL approval) sending professional communications
- Organizational work product: producing documents, reports, proposals, and communications on behalf of the authenticated executive

# 3. Prohibited Use

The following uses of NYXOR are prohibited and constitute a material breach of the Terms of Service:

**3.1 Prohibited by All Users**

- Using NYXOR to produce content intended to deceive, defraud, or manipulate any individual or organization
- Using NYXOR agents to impersonate another individual or organization without authorization
- Using NYXOR to process personal data of third parties without their knowledge and a lawful basis for processing
- Attempting to circumvent, disable, or exploit any HITL governance control
- Using NYXOR to produce content that violates applicable law, including defamation, hate speech, or harassment
- Extracting, exporting, or sharing NYXOR outputs in ways that expose third-party personal data
- Using NYXOR to produce content for industries or activities prohibited by Valtara's acceptable use policy (weapons, exploitation, illegal services)

**3.2 Prohibited by Enterprise Administrators**

- Configuring NYXOR to access data belonging to executives who have not provided informed consent
- Using administrator access to read or export executive content without the executive's knowledge
- Disabling or modifying HITL governance controls without documented authorization from the Chief AI Officer

# 4. User Obligations

| Obligation            | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| HITL responsibility   | The executive is responsible for reviewing all AI-generated content before approving it for external use. Approval constitutes the executive's endorsement of the content. |
| Accuracy verification | Executives must not rely solely on AI-generated research or analysis for material decisions without independent verification where the stakes warrant it.                  |
| Credential security   | Executives are responsible for maintaining the security of their NYXOR account credentials and integration authorizations.                                                 |
| Scope of delegation   | Executives may only delegate tasks within their own authority. NYXOR agents must not be used to perform actions the executive could not lawfully perform themselves.       |
| Reporting             | Executives must report any AI output that appears harmful, biased, or in violation of this policy to Valtara AI via the designated reporting channel.                      |

# 5. Data Use Commitments

| Commitment                  | Detail                                                                                                                |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No advertising use          | Executive data is never used to serve advertisements or shared with advertising platforms.                            |
| No resale                   | Executive data is never sold, rented, or brokered to third parties.                                                   |
| No training without consent | AI models are not trained on executive data without explicit, documented, opt-in consent. Default is no training use. |
| Minimum necessary access    | NYXOR requests only the minimum OAuth scopes required for stated functionality at the time of connection.             |
| Right to withdraw           | Executives may revoke any integration or delete their account at any time with immediate effect on data processing.   |

# 6. Policy Enforcement

**6.1 Monitoring**

Valtara monitors aggregate system usage patterns for compliance with this policy. Individual executive content is not monitored by Valtara staff except: (a) in response to a reported incident with the executive's consent; (b) as required by applicable law.

**6.2 Violation Response**

| Violation Type                                                 | Response                                                                                                                          |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First confirmed violation — minor                              | Written notice; policy re-acknowledgment required; incident logged                                                                |
| Repeated or material violation                                 | Service suspension pending review; incident documented                                                                            |
| Severe violation (fraud, illegal content, HITL bypass attempt) | Immediate service termination; data preserved for legal compliance; incident reported to relevant authorities if legally required |

**6.3 Reporting**

Policy violations or concerns may be reported to: fcogbogu@gmail.com. Reports are treated confidentially. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is prohibited.

# 7. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually and upon any material change to NYXOR capabilities, applicable regulation, or a material incident. All amendments require Chief AI Officer approval and are communicated to active users with 30 days notice before taking effect.
