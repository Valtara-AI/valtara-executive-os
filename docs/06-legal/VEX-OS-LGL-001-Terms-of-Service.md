## 2. What VEX-OS Is

VEX-OS is an AI-powered executive operating system comprising an onboarding process that builds a personalized profile of your role and working style, a configurable workforce of AI agents provisioned to that profile, and a dashboard through which you review, approve, edit, or reject everything those agents produce before it takes effect in the outside world.

**Human-in-the-loop is architectural, not optional.** No agent operating under your account sends an email, posts a message, modifies your calendar, or takes any other externally-visible action without your prior approval, recorded and enforced at the database level. This is a structural property of the Service, not a configuration you can disable, and it is central to what you are agreeing to when you connect an integration or activate an agent.

3.2. VEX-OS authenticates via your Google or Microsoft identity (OAuth). You are responsible for the security of that underlying account.

3.3. You may invite a Delegate (e.g., an executive assistant or chief of staff) to review and act on items in your HITL queue on your behalf. You are responsible for that Delegate's access and remain accountable for actions they approve.

## 4. Subscriptions, Billing, and Trials

4.1. VEX-OS is offered on subscription tiers (currently Starter, Pro, and Enterprise), each with defined limits on active agents, connected integrations, Delegate seats, and monthly usage, as described at checkout and in-product.

4.2. New subscriptions include a 14-day trial. Unless cancelled before the trial ends, your payment method will be charged for the selected tier when the trial expires.

4.3. Billing is processed by Stripe, Inc. Valtara does not store your payment card details. Subscriptions renew automatically each billing period until cancelled. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through the in-product billing portal.

4.4. Each tier includes a monthly allowance of AI usage. If that allowance is exhausted before the billing period ends, new agent tasks will not run until the next period or until you upgrade — VEX-OS will tell you clearly when this happens rather than charging you unexpectedly.

4.5. Fees are non-refundable except as required by applicable law or as Valtara expressly agrees in writing.

## 5. Connected Integrations (Google, Microsoft, Slack, PandaDoc)

You can disconnect any integration at any time; doing so revokes VEX-OS's access to that account going forward.

5.2. VEX-OS requests only the minimum access (OAuth scopes) required for the features you use, and does not bulk-download your mailbox, calendar, or message history — data is retrieved contextually, for the specific task an agent is performing.

5.3. VEX-OS is not responsible for the availability, security practices, or terms of the third-party platforms you connect (Google, Microsoft, Slack, PandaDoc). Your use of those platforms remains governed by your agreements with them.

## 7. AI-Generated Content

7.1. VEX-OS uses third-party large language model providers (currently Anthropic by default; the Service is architected to be provider-agnostic and may use others) to generate drafts, summaries, and proposed actions. AI-generated output can be inaccurate or inappropriate for its context.

7.2. **You are responsible for reviewing and approving everything before it is sent or acted upon.** VEX-OS's HITL governance model exists precisely so that no AI output reaches the outside world without your review, but the final judgment on any approved item is yours.

7.3. Task prompts and relevant context (which may include content from your connected integrations) are sent to the configured LLM provider to generate a response. Valtara's agreements with LLM providers prohibit using your data to train their models without your explicit separate consent.
